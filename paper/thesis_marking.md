# Marking Rubric >70% distinction
- Area of research and research gap identification : Clear, strong, and focused. 
- State of the art and related work: Present, relevant, critical and sufficiently broad.
- Methodology : Unambiguous, fully specified, and reproducible end-to-end method with explicit assumptions and audit trail. 
- Experiments and evidence : Correct, with baselines/control, statistical significance, and details to reproduce the results
- Discussion and conclusions : Impact discussed, claims supported, broad scope of application (e.g., tested at scale/production)

# Structure need to be 
- Abstract : What, why, how, key result -- in 150 - 250 words
- Introduction : Context -> problem -> gap -> your contribution
- Related Work : What exists, why it is insufficient -- not a catalogue.
- Methods : Reproducible enough for a peer to replicate your work
- Results : Objective data only - no interpretation here/
- Discussion : interpret results, acknowledge limitations, situate in literature
- Conclusion : Answer rthe research question. No new information

# Pres-submission checklist
- Is there a single, clear research question stated in the introduction?
- Does every claim in the discussion appear in the results?
- Have you cited the papers closest to your work, including those you outperform?
- Can someone reproduce your experiment from the methods section alone?
- Does your abstract contatin at least one concrete, quantitateive result?
- Have you read the target journal's scope and formatting guidelines?
- Has a colleague outside your subfield read and undestood the paper?

All of the checklist should be checked before submit
